# Diary of a Sales Agent: Harrogate Summer Show & The Christmas Countdown Begins!

*By Dave Langdon, East Anglian Sales Ltd*

---

## The Harrogate Hangover (The Good Kind!)

Well, well, well... if you'd told me a few years ago that I'd be writing about trade shows with the same enthusiasm as a kid on Christmas morning, I'd have laughed you out of the room. But here we are, and the Harrogate Summer Show has left me buzzing like a bee in a flower shop!

The show was absolutely crackers (and I'm not just talking about the free biscuits in the exhibitor lounge). The energy was electric, the networking was next-level, and I'm pretty sure I shook more hands than a politician on election day. But the real story? The products. Oh, the glorious products!

## Ohh Deer, What Have We Here?

Let's talk about Ohh Deer's new launches because, quite frankly, they're the gift that keeps on giving. Their new A7 Mini Notebooks are so cute, I'm half expecting them to start talking to me. Perfect stocking fillers that won't break the bank but will definitely break the monotony of traditional gift displays.

And speaking of breaking tradition...

## Paper Salad: The Christmas Card Revolution

Now, I know what you're thinking. "Dave, Christmas cards are Christmas cards. What's the big deal?" Well, my friends, let me introduce you to Paper Salad's Christmas collection – the equivalent of adding a disco ball to a library.

While traditional Christmas cards are lovely (and I'm not knocking them – they pay my mortgage), Paper Salad brings something different to the table. Their designs are like having a conversation with your cool aunt who always knows the latest trends. They add that splash of colour and personality that makes customers stop, look, and inevitably buy.

I'm telling you, when you see a traditional greeting card display next to one with Paper Salad's vibrant designs, it's like watching a black and white film suddenly burst into Technicolor. The difference is that dramatic.

## The Q4 Gift Game Changer

Here's where it gets interesting. We're heading into Q4, and if you're not thinking about gifting, you're basically playing retail roulette. And let me tell you about a little gem that's going to make your life easier – the Global Journey Wooden Keyring Stand.

This isn't just a keyring stand. This is a stocking filler superhero. It's practical, it's beautiful, and it's the kind of gift that makes people think you put actual thought into their present (even if you bought it in bulk). Plus, it's wooden, so it's got that whole "sustainable" vibe that customers love these days.

## The Lucky Sales Agent's Trifecta

I have to pinch myself sometimes because I genuinely feel like I've won the sales agent lottery. Museums and Galleries, Ohh Deer, and Paper Salad – it's like having the three musketeers of the gift industry in my portfolio.

**Museums and Galleries** brings that sophisticated, cultured vibe that makes customers feel like they're supporting something meaningful. **Ohh Deer** brings the fun, the humour, and the "oh my goodness, I need this" factor. And **Paper Salad**? Well, they bring the colour, the personality, and the ability to make any display pop.

It's like having a perfectly balanced meal – you've got your protein (Museums and Galleries), your carbs (Ohh Deer), and your vegetables (Paper Salad). Wait, that metaphor got away from me. Let's just say they complement each other beautifully.

## The Christmas Card Conundrum

Let's address the elephant in the room – Christmas cards. I know, I know, it's July, and you're probably thinking "Dave, slow down, I haven't even had my summer holiday yet." But here's the thing: Christmas cards are the bread and butter of our industry, and the early bird gets the worm (or in this case, the customer).

The beauty of having such diverse brands is that you can cater to every type of customer. Traditional? Museums and Galleries has you covered. Quirky and fun? Ohh Deer is your go-to. Colourful and contemporary? Paper Salad is the answer.

It's like having a Swiss Army knife of greeting cards – there's a tool for every job.

## The Road Ahead

As I write this, I'm planning my next round of visits to my lovely customers across East Anglia. Suffolk, Norfolk, Essex, and Cambridgeshire – you're all in for a treat because I'm coming armed with samples, catalogues, and enough enthusiasm to power a small village.

The Harrogate show has reminded me why I love this job. It's not just about selling products; it's about building relationships, understanding what makes customers tick, and helping them create displays that make their customers smile.

## Final Thoughts (And a Shameless Plug)

So, what's the takeaway from this sales agent's diary entry? Well, if you're a retailer reading this, I'd love to chat about how we can make your displays more exciting, your Christmas cards more colourful, and your Q4 sales more successful.

And if you're a fellow sales agent reading this, well, I hope this gives you a chuckle and maybe even a few ideas. After all, we're all in this together, trying to make the world a more colourful, gift-filled place.

Now, if you'll excuse me, I need to go and practice my "excited about Christmas cards in July" face. It's a work in progress.
