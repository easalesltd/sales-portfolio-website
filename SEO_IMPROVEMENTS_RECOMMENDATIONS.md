# SEO Improvements Specific to Your Sales Agent Business

Based on your current site structure, here are actionable SEO improvements tailored to your greeting card and giftware sales agent business:

## 🎯 High-Impact Improvements

### 1. **Location-Specific Landing Pages** (Highest Priority)
**Why:** You have keywords targeting each county, but no dedicated pages. This is a huge opportunity.

**Create pages for:**
- `/suffolk` - "Greeting Card Sales Agent Suffolk"
- `/norfolk` - "Greeting Card Sales Agent Norfolk"  
- `/essex` - "Greeting Card Sales Agent Essex"
- `/cambridgeshire` - "Greeting Card Sales Agent Cambridgeshire"
- `/hertfordshire` - "Greeting Card Sales Agent Hertfordshire"

**Each page should include:**
- H1: "Greeting Card & Gift Sales Agent in [County]"
- Content about serving retailers in that specific county
- List of major towns/cities you cover
- Local testimonials (if available)
- FAQ section specific to that area
- Structured data with county-specific targeting

**Expected impact:** Capture location-specific searches that currently go to competitors

---
